How should the "shape combinations" work?

A chord (say C) can be fingered in several places. Across a progression (Bm→C→G…) there are many ways to traverse the neck. How do you want to explore them? Click the one that matches your goal.

A

Per-chord cycling

One fretboard. Step Next/Prev through the chords; at each chord, flip ◀▶ through that chord's own shapes. A side list shows the current chord's alternatives. You discover movements by choosing as you go.

B

Full-sequence paths (list of combinations)

Pre-compute several complete ways to play the whole progression — ranked by least hand-movement / neck region — and list them. Pick one, and the fretboard animates through it step by step with the previous chord dimmed.

C

Both — pick a path, then fine-tune per chord

Start from a listed full-sequence path (B), then while stepping you can still flip ◀▶ to swap an individual chord's shape (A). Richest, but more UI and more to build.

My lean: B best matches "list different possible combinations … visualise the different movements" — each path is one coherent way to play the whole thing. A is simpler; C is the most flexible but heaviest.
